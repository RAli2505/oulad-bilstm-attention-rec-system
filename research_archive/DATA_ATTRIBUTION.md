Dataset: **Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD)**.

Creators: **Jakub Kuzilek, Martin Hlosta, Zdenek Zdrahal**; The Open University.

Reference: Kuzilek, J., Hlosta, M. & Zdrahal, Z. Open University Learning Analytics dataset. *Scientific Data* **4**, 170171 (2017). [https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.171](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.171).

The dataset is distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as stated on the [official dataset page](https://research.stem.open.ac.uk/ouanalyse/open-dataset-more/), checked on 2026-11-01.

`snapshot_20261101/artifacts/oulad_raw.zip` preserves the seven local CSV inputs used by the archived experiments. The archive creation process did not modify their contents; SHA256 values match the input hashes in all three run manifests. Packaging into ZIP does not change CSV contents. Feature transformations, preprocessing objects and derived predictions are stored separately with their experiment artifacts.

This data license applies to the OULAD data; it does not assign a new license to third-party architectures, dependencies or the repository's own code. Preserve this attribution and the license link when redistributing the dataset or a copy of this research archive.
